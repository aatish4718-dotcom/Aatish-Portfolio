02 / 05 — Position

I am an urban planner who also works in visual communication, photography and film.

That combination is the point.

The planning gives the work a question to answer; the visual practice gives the answer somewhere to land. A ward-level sanitation campaign, a nineteen-page conference brochure and a film about a colony of puppeteers facing redevelopment are the same job done at different distances.
